Indian Premier League

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India, founded in 2008 by the BCCI. It features franchise teams representing different cities and is one of the most popular and richest cricket leagues in the world. The IPL brings together top international and Indian players, combining high-intensity cricket with entertainment, making it one of the most watched sporting events globally.
